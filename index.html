TrueTrack

truetrack.in

TrueTrack is a real-time tracking platform for fleets, field teams and high-value assets.

It brings live location, geofencing and route history into one dashboard, so operations teams always know where things stand — without chasing drivers for updates or piecing together spreadsheets after the fact. It's built for teams that manage vehicles or equipment across multiple sites, where a single missed handover or an idle truck can cost real money.

  • Live GPS tracking — every vehicle and asset on a real-time map.
  • Geofencing & alerts — instant notice when something enters or exits a zone.
  • Route history & playback — replay any trip to review stops and idle time.
  • Reports & analytics — utilization and performance reports your team can act on.
Visit truetrack.in

BlogSpeed

blogspeed.io

BlogSpeed is a lightweight blogging and content platform built for speed — fast to write in, fast to load, fast to rank.

It strips away the bloated editors and slow themes that come with most publishing tools, leaving a platform built purely for getting words onto the web quickly. It suits teams and creators who publish often and don't want performance to be an ongoing project of its own.

  • Instant-load pages — built to load in a fraction of a second, on any connection.
  • Distraction-free editor — write and format without fighting the tool.
  • Built-in SEO — clean markup and metadata handled automatically.
  • Team publishing — roles, drafts and review flows for teams that publish together.
Visit blogspeed.io

SkillPact

skillpact.co.in

SkillPact connects learners with industry-aligned courses, assessments and certifications.

Course content is shaped with input from hiring teams, so what someone learns maps directly to what actually gets them hired — rather than a generic syllabus that sounds impressive but doesn't match real job requirements. Learners work at their own pace, and employers get a certificate they can trust and verify.

  • Industry-mapped courses — curriculum built around real job requirements.
  • Skill assessments — verify what you know, not just what you've watched.
  • Verified certification — shareable certificates employers can independently check.
  • Progress tracking — one dashboard for learning across every course.
Visit skillpact.co.in

Tabzy

tabzy.in

Tabzy is a digital tab and billing platform for restaurants, cafés and bars.

Guests open a tab with a QR code, order as they go, and settle up — split or whole — without flagging down staff for the check every time. It's built for venues that want faster table turns and fewer billing mistakes, without ripping out the POS system they already use.

  • QR-based ordering — guests browse the menu and order straight from the table.
  • Live running tabs — every order lands on an open tab in real time.
  • Split & settle — guests split the bill any way they like and pay from their phones.
  • Staff dashboard — floor staff see every open tab and order status at a glance.
Visit tabzy.in
