Who we are

A technology services partner you can build a company on.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about how CEEC partners with businesses worldwide.

What technology services do you offer?

We offer end-to-end software engineering, cloud & infrastructure migrations, technology consulting, and managed support to keep your systems running smoothly after launch.

Do you work with clients globally?

Yes, we deliberately design for a global reality. Our clients are spread across time zones, primarily in China, South Asia, the Middle East, Europe, and North America.

Can you build custom software for my business?

Absolutely. Our software engineering team handles the complete design and development of custom web and mobile applications from prototype to launch.

How do you approach a new client project?

We start with a Discovery phase to understand your constraints and success metrics. Then, we move deliberately through Design, Build, and Support, ensuring iterative delivery with no surprises.

Do you provide ongoing support after a project is finished?

Yes, once your product is live, our team stays reachable for proactive monitoring, bug fixes, and continuous feature improvements under our Managed Support service.
